Swap WETH is a non-custodial interface for wrapping ETH into WETH and trading WETH through public DEX contracts. It never holds your assets or private keys; every wrap and swap is a transaction you sign from your own wallet. Nothing here is financial, investment or legal advice.

Privacy policy

This interface is a static front-end that routes swaps through public smart contracts. It has no accounts and does not request personal information.

  • No sign-up, email or password is collected by this interface.
  • Wallet connections happen in your browser; your private keys never leave your wallet.
  • Public blockchain data (addresses, balances, transactions) is read from the networks and is inherently public.
  • Third-party services you connect (wallets, RPC providers, DEX routers, explorers) have their own privacy practices.

Terms of use

By using this interface you accept full responsibility for your transactions and acknowledge the following:

  • Wrapping is 1:1, but swapping tokens carries risk, including price impact, slippage and smart-contract risk.
  • Quotes and routes depend on live liquidity and can change before your transaction confirms.
  • You pay network gas plus any aggregator fee reflected in the quote; ERC-20 tokens need a one-time approval.
  • Many fake tokens imitate real ones — always verify the token contract address before trading.
  • The interface is provided “as is”, without warranties of any kind, and may contain errors or become unavailable.

Verify the network, token and amount in your wallet before signing, and never share your seed phrase or private keys.
