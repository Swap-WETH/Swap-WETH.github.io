What is WETH

What WETH is

Wrapped Ether, an ERC-20 twin of ETH

WETH is Wrapped Ether: an ERC-20 token that always equals one ETH. Native ETH predates the ERC-20 standard and does not follow it, so it cannot be traded directly in most pools or passed to most contracts. Wrapping ETH mints an equal amount of WETH that behaves like any other ERC-20 token.

Why it exists

Most DEX pools, lending markets and smart contracts are written for ERC-20 tokens. WETH lets your ETH meet that interface without changing its value, so you can supply it to a pool, use it as collateral, or trade it like a token while staying 1:1 with ETH.

Wrap vs swap

Wrapping is not swapping

Two different actions get mixed up. Knowing which one you are doing tells you whether to expect a price, slippage, or just gas.

In practice

Turning ETH into WETH, or WETH back into ETH, is a wrap at a guaranteed 1:1 rate. Trading WETH for USDT, USDC or any other token is a real swap routed through a DEX, priced by the market with slippage.

How to swap

How to swap WETH

Route and quote

A DEX or aggregator searches pools for the best price, then shows the expected output, the price impact and a slippage tolerance. You confirm the quote before anything is signed.

Step by step

  1. Connect your wallet and set WETH as the sell token and the token you want as the buy token.
  2. Enter an amount and review the quote, route and estimated gas.
  3. Approve WETH once if this is your first swap of it from this wallet.
  4. Sign the swap; it settles on-chain and the bought token lands in your wallet.

If you only need WETH itself, wrap ETH instead of swapping — it is 1:1 and skips price impact.

Fees & gas

What a WETH swap costs

Gas

Every step — wrapping, approving and swapping — is a separate Ethereum transaction paid in native ETH. See how gas works.

Trading cost

A swap also carries price impact, your slippage tolerance, and any pool or aggregator fee, all reflected in the quoted output. Wrapping ETH into WETH has no trading fee — only gas.

Keep ETH for gas

Holding WETH does not pay for gas. Keep a little native ETH in the same wallet or you will not be able to approve or sign a swap.

Security

Staying safe

Non-custodial by design

You connect your own wallet and sign every transaction yourself. A swap interface routes and quotes — it never holds your funds or keys.

Before you sign

  • Verify the token contract address; fake WETH and lookalike stablecoins are common.
  • Approve only what you need, and revoke old ERC-20 allowances you no longer use.
  • Check the quote, price impact and slippage before confirming.
  • Smart contracts can hold bugs, so risk is never zero.
FAQ

Swap WETH FAQ

Is WETH the same as ETH?

WETH and ETH are worth the same and convert 1:1, but they are not interchangeable in code. ETH is the native coin of the network and is not an ERC-20 token, while WETH is an ERC-20 wrapper that smart contracts and DEXs can handle like any other token.

Do I wrap or swap to get WETH?

It depends on what you start with. Turning ETH into WETH is a wrap at a fixed 1:1 rate, with no price impact. Turning another token into WETH is a market swap routed through a DEX, with a quote and slippage.

Why do I need to approve WETH before a swap?

WETH is an ERC-20 token, so the router needs a one-time allowance to move it on your behalf. Native ETH needs no approval. The approval is a separate transaction that costs gas before your first WETH swap.

Do I still need ETH if I hold WETH?

Yes. Gas on Ethereum is always paid in native ETH, so keep a small ETH balance even when your trading funds are in WETH, or you will not be able to sign the swap.

Can I unwrap WETH back to ETH at any time?

Yes. Unwrapping burns WETH and returns native ETH 1:1, minus gas. There is no trading fee or slippage because it is a wrap, not a market swap.

Is there slippage when wrapping ETH into WETH?

No. Wrapping and unwrapping are fixed 1:1 conversions, so slippage and price impact do not apply. Those only affect market swaps where WETH is traded against a different token.
