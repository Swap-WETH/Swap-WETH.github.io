What is Swap WETH?

Uniswap is a wallet-connected trading app for token swaps on Ethereum and supported networks. WETH is the ERC-20 representation of ether described by ethereum.org.

How Swap WETH works

  1. Open the routeUse the app button to open Uniswap with WETH preselected on Ethereum.
  2. Connect walletChoose the wallet holding your WETH and keep native ETH available for gas.
  3. Review quoteCheck the output token, network, price impact, slippage settings, and recipient before continuing.
  4. Sign only in walletConfirm the transaction from your wallet after verifying the app domain and token details.

Before you swap

Prefilled app path

Uniswap supports swap links with token parameters through its custom linking docs, so visitors can land closer to the action.

ERC-20 liquidity flow

WETH follows the ERC-20 token model, which lets DeFi apps handle ether-like value through standard token approvals and transfers.

Contract verification

For Ethereum mainnet, verify Wrapped Ether at Etherscan before approving or signing.

Swap WETH FAQ

How do I swap weth safely?

Open the Uniswap app from the official app domain, verify WETH and the output token, review price impact and gas, then sign only the transaction you expect.

Is swap weth the same as wrapping ETH?

No. Wrapping converts ETH into WETH through the WETH contract; swapping trades WETH for another token through a liquidity route.

What network should I use to swap weth?

Use the network where your WETH balance exists. On Ethereum mainnet, the WETH contract is 0xC02aaA39b223FE8D0A0e5C4F27eAD9083C756Cc2.

Do I need ETH to swap weth?

Yes. On Ethereum mainnet, gas is paid in native ETH, not WETH, so keep enough ETH in the wallet for transaction fees.

Why use Uniswap to swap weth?

Uniswap is a widely used wallet-based DEX app, and its interface can open with prefilled swap parameters while still requiring you to review and sign in your own wallet.
