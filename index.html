FAQ

Common questions

01What does Swap WETH mean?

Swap WETH means exchanging Wrapped Ether for another asset, or swapping another token back into WETH. WETH follows the token format many DeFi apps use for liquidity pools and routing.

02Is this a custodial exchange?

No. The app is built for non-custodial wallet use. You connect a wallet such as MetaMask, review the route, and sign the transaction yourself.

03Which assets and chains are supported?

The app focuses on WETH routes across supported EVM networks, including ETH pairs, BTC routes, stablecoin routes, and other available DeFi assets. See the current list on the supported chains page.

04How long does a WETH swap take?

Timing depends on the selected network, route liquidity, and confirmation conditions. The app displays live route details before you approve and sign.

05What does it cost to swap WETH?

Costs depend on the route, token approval needs, and the network fee at the time you trade. The app shows the live quote and network cost before confirmation.

06How should I check safety before swapping?

Review the token, route, approval request, receiving asset, and wallet prompt. For market context, compare decentralized exchange liquidity on sources such as Uniswap before signing.
